# **HR Workforce Intelligence & Attrition Analytics**

A Power BI project focused on analyzing workforce distribution, employee attrition, tenure, performance reviews, and compensation using employee data.

## **Objectives**

- Analyze workforce distribution.
- Understand employee attrition patterns.
- Compare departments.
- Explore employee tenure and review outcomes.
- Analyze compensation patterns.

## **Dataset**

- Records: 6,000 employees
- Format: Excel
- Source: Workforce Employee Attrition dataset

## **Dashboard Pages**

1. Workforce Overview
2. Attrition Analysis
3. Employee Experience
4. Compensation & Career Growth

## **Tools Used**

- Power BI
- Power Query
- DAX
- Excel

## **Key Insights**

- Overall workforce and termination patterns were analyzed.
- Departmental termination rates were compared.
- Tenure differences between active and terminated employees were explored.
- Salary and review scores were compared across employee groups.

## **Recommendations**

- Investigate departments with higher termination rates.
- Improve employee retention strategies.
- Review career growth and compensation patterns.
- Monitor workforce KPIs regularly.

## **Limitations**

- Historical dataset.
- Missing values in some fields.
- Findings do not establish causes of attrition.
